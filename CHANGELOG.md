# Changelog

## 0.9.0 – 2026-10-09

First public release (previously developed as "DCS-Kampagne").

- Real-time dynamic campaign: Caucasus and three Persian Gulf scenarios
- AI air tasking orders for both sides, ground war along several axes, logistics by road, rail and sea, war economy
- Fog of war with radars, recon, pre-war intel and decoys
- Five-step flight planner, mission generator, debriefing from `dcs.log`
- New campaign dialog: theater, side, aircraft per nation (incl. F-14A/B/B(U)), carrier, start time (default 10:00)
- Multiplayer planning in the browser, optional server hook (slot locking, automatic next mission)
- Splash Damage script support (recommended, download separately) and optional DCS-ATC (voice ATC, AWACS, JTAC/FAC(A) on CAS missions)
- New name and icon: Warclock
